Reset lighting

Restart
The device restarts itself after a hostname or hotspot address change, and after a firmware update. There is no restart button, on this page or on the original.
Factory reset
Erases every setting the device holds: Wi-Fi, hotspot, printer binding, hostname, language, lighting. The device restarts as new. There is no undo.
